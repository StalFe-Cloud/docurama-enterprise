## 5. Setup order (runbook)

1. **Zone baseline** — publish the A/CNAME hosts (section 2) at the registrar;
   the full BIND zone is attached as `docurama.eu.zone`.
2. **Mailbox P0s first** — create `notifications@`, `admin@` (or the forwarder to
   docurama@voiria.com), `postmaster@`, `abuse@`, `privacy@`, `security@`.
3. **Relay & SPF/DKIM** — connect the EU relay, publish SPF + both DKIM selectors,
   then keep DMARC at `p=quarantine` until alignment reports are clean (watch
   `dmarc@docurama.eu`), then move to `p=reject`.
4. **PTR/rDNS** — set `51.75.124.20 → mail.docurama.eu` at the hosting provider so
   the SMTP HELO matches; mismatched PTR is the fastest route to spam folders.
5. **MTA-STS + TLS-RPT** — publish the TXT records and the policy file over HTTPS.
6. **CAA** — restrict certificate issuance to Let's Encrypt with iodef reporting.
7. **Business aliases** — `support@`, `sales@`, `billing@`, `contact@`, `legal@`.
8. **Registry data** — replace SIREN, VAT, share capital and phone placeholders
   from the company Kbis; they flow into the legal footer, invoices and DSAR responses.

## 6. Compliance notes (GDPR / eIDAS)

- `privacy@docurama.eu` is the DSAR intake required for GDPR Arts. 13/14/30
  transparency and one-month response duties; DSAR cases are tracked in the
  admin console under Legal & DSAR.
- The audit chain, retention policies and legal holds in the platform satisfy
  eIDAS evidence requirements for advanced electronic signatures (AdES/PAdES).
- EU data residency: all sub-domains terminate on EU-West origins; no
  sub-processor outside the EEA is involved in the signing pipeline.
