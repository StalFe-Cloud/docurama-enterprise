eSignature pricing in Europe tends toward two failure modes: enterprise opacity ("talk to sales" before you can see a euro), or metered anxiety (a per-envelope fee that makes you think twice before sending the NDA that protects you). We built Docurama's pricing to be boring in the best way: predictable, generous with time, and honest about what you are buying.

## Five plans, one rule: buy seats, not surprises

| Plan | Price | Seats | Envelopes / month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo | €12 | 1 | 30 |
| Starter | €29 | 3 | 100 |
| Business | €79 | 10 | 250 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom |

**Solo is sized for individuals; Starter adds capacity and collaboration.** Solo is €12/month for one seat and 30 envelopes. Starter is €29/month for three seats and 100 envelopes. Both include rollover and 35 sending days per billing month.

Signers never cost anything and never need an account — you could run your entire supplier network through a Solo plan. Seats are for people who *send* and *manage*.

## 35 sending days a month — weekends are on us

A calendar month has 28–31 days, and roughly 8–9 of them are weekends when nobody signs anything meaningful. Charging a "monthly" allowance while the clock runs through Saturdays felt quietly dishonest, so Solo and Starter allowances are framed over **35 sending days**: the working days of the month, plus our weekends on us. You get the whole month you actually operate in.

## Rollover: a quiet month is never a lost month

Unused envelopes roll into the next period when your term renews, capped at one monthly allowance. Use 20 of Solo's 30 envelopes, and the next period starts with 40. The cap prevents unused allowances from accumulating indefinitely.

## Terms are a discount, never a lock-in

Commit to 3, 6 or 12 months and the whole term is discounted by 5, 10 or 20 percent respectively. Monthly remains the default option.

## What every plan includes, always

Unlimited signers and recipients. The public verification portal. Evidence packs with offline verification. Envelope rollover. Branded signing pages. The audit ledger. These are not upsells — they are the reason the product exists, and a Solo founder's contract deserves the same proof as a 50-seat enterprise's.

## The Enterprise tier is a conversation, not a maze

SSO, BYO certificates, self-hosting or on-premises deployment, custom volumes, QSCD integration, 21 CFR Part 11 workflows — these genuinely vary per organization, so they are priced per reality. The [enterprise pipeline](/enterprise) reaches an engineer-led pilot process with a fixed scope, not a lead-scoring funnel.

That is the whole philosophy: prices you can calculate in your head, allowances that respect how months actually work, and evidence that is never paywalled.
